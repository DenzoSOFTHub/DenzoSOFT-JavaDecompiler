# BUG-2026-0122: statements displaced out of the try region

**Type**: Bug
**Status**: RELEASED
**Severity**: HIGH
**Created**: 2026-09-05
**Resolved**: 2026-09-07 (split)

## Description
A statement whose bytecode lies inside a protected range could be emitted AFTER the catch clause.

## Resolution: split
Work in v1.13.0 fixed this for regions without line information and established that the line-bearing
case is a materially different problem — it trades fabricated catch clauses against lost real ones, and
needs its own measurement before it can be touched. The two halves were therefore separated:

| Item | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [BUG-2026-0125](BUG-2026-0125.md) | regions WITHOUT line information (`-g:none`) | CLOSED, released in v1.13.0 |
| [BUG-2026-0126](../../tracking/items/BUG-2026-0126.md) | classes WITH line information | OPEN |

This item is closed and carries no content of its own; see the two above.
