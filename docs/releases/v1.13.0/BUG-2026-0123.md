# BUG-2026-0123: pattern switches left as a raw typeSwitch dispatch

**Type**: Bug
**Status**: RELEASED
**Severity**: HIGH
**Created**: 2026-09-06
**Resolved**: 2026-09-07 (split)

## Description
7 of the 3,376 JDK 25 `java.base` classes emitted the raw `SwitchBootstraps.typeSwitch` dispatch with
every arm value dropped.

## Resolution: split
Measuring the 7 showed three distinct shapes, not one. Two were an over-broad guard and were fixed;
the third is flow-builder work of a different order. The halves were therefore separated:

| Item | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [BUG-2026-0127](BUG-2026-0127.md) | constant labels and array-class labels (2 of 7) | CLOSED, released in v1.13.0 |
| [BUG-2026-0128](../../tracking/items/BUG-2026-0128.md) | guarded arms with an index restart (5 of 7) | OPEN |

This item is closed and carries no content of its own; see the two above.
