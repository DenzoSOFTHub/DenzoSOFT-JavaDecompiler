## Description

The exception table before BUG-2026-0068 drops handler-protection entries. Such an entry can also be the second FRAGMENT of an outer try that encloses an inner try/catch: [62,102)->114 and [103,111)->114 where 103 is the inner handler (javax.sound.sampled.AudioSystem.getMixer). Dropping it shrank the outer span to 102, and the BUG-2026-0189 rule then pushed the inner loop (last pc 108) out of the try.
